Burak for football: proof of concept

Burak reads a squad’s routine blood tests across a season and turns them into early signals for performance and medical staff: muscle recovery, overreaching, iron, hydration in the heat, energy availability, bone health and illness.

  • Illustrative only. Players, results and dates are fictional. Ranges and rules are drawn from published sports-science literature and are illustrative until reviewed by the club’s medical team.
  • Personal baselines. Each player is compared with his own earlier results, not only with population ranges.
  • Staff decide. Burak flags and explains. It does not diagnose, clear players to play, or predict injuries for an individual.
